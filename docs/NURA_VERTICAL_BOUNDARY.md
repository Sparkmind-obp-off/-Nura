# NuraVertical Product Boundary

## Definition

NuraVertical is the Nura business line for needs rooted in real-world business operations and specific vertical workflows.

## Initial Discovery Verticals

- Nura Barber
- Nura Cafe

These are starting examples for validation, not a decision to build complete vertical platforms immediately.

## Vertical Qualification

A vertical should have evidence of:

1. Real users or operators.
2. Repeated activity.
3. Recurring problems.
4. Observable workflows.
5. Meaningful willingness to use, pay, or adopt.
6. Measurable outcomes that Nuralabs can help execute or verify.

## Productization Flow

```text
Real Activity
→ Problem
→ Workflow
→ Validation
→ Pilot
→ Repeatability
→ Productization
```

## Example: Barber

Possible operational needs include customer records, booking, staff schedules, service catalog, customer history, daily operations, and reporting.

These are discovery hypotheses only. Actual scope must come from observed demand.

## Example: Cafe

Possible operational needs include menu management, ordering, inventory, staff operations, promotions, and reporting.

Again, these are discovery hypotheses rather than predetermined product requirements.

## Relationship with NuraDigital

A reusable cross-industry digital workflow belongs in NuraDigital.

A workflow whose value depends materially on vertical-specific terminology, data, rules, or operations belongs in NuraVertical.

## Guardrail

Never create a vertical because the market sounds large. Create it because real activity and repeated demand demonstrate that a vertical-specific solution is justified.
