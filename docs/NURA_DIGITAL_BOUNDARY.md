# NuraDigital Product Boundary

## Definition

NuraDigital is the Nura business line for real digital needs that can be solved through reusable digital workflows and execution.

## Typical Demand

- Website and landing page work
- Business applications
- Automation
- Software/code work
- API and system integration
- Dashboards and internal tools
- Digital design/assets
- Digital operations

These are examples, not a commitment to build every category immediately.

## Execution Pattern

```text
Brief
→ Plan
→ Build
→ Run
→ Verify
→ Package
→ Deliver
```

Nuralabs Core owns the execution machinery. NuraDigital owns the business surface, offer, customer context, and delivery experience.

## Web as a Wedge

Web work can be an early commercial wedge because it is easy to demonstrate and has broad demand. It must not become the identity of NuraDigital or Nura as a whole.

## Productization Rule

Do not build a large product merely because a service category exists. Productize repeated, validated workflows after observing real demand and successful delivery.

## Boundary with NuraVertical

A generic reusable problem belongs in NuraDigital.

A problem that depends materially on industry-specific terminology, rules, data, or operations belongs in NuraVertical.

## Non-Goals

- Do not become a generic feature marketplace before demand is proven.
- Do not duplicate Nuralabs Core.
- Do not claim automation where execution is simulated.
- Do not expand into many product categories without evidence.
